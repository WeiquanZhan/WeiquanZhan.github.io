<div class="mine-home-bio">
  <p>
    Dr. Weiquan Zhan is a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the
    <a href="https://www.ntu.edu.sg/cee" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">School of Civil and Environmental Engineering</a>
    at <a href="https://www.ntu.edu.sg/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Nanyang Technological University (NTU)</a>, Singapore.
    Previously, he was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at
    <a href="https://www.univ-lorraine.fr/en/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Universit&eacute; de Lorraine</a>, France.
    He received his Ph.D. in Interdisciplinary Science from the
    <a href="https://www.uaslp.mx/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Autonomous University of San Luis Potos&iacute;</a>, Mexico, in 2025, following his M.S. and B.S. degrees from Wuhan University of Technology, China.
  </p>

  <p>
    His research focuses on <strong>mineral interface engineering</strong> for sustainable resource recovery and low-carbon materials.
    By integrating <strong>multiscale computational modeling</strong>, electrochemistry, catalysis, and mineral surface engineering,
    he investigates how interface structure and hydration regulate ion transport, selective adsorption, interfacial bonding, and chemical reactions.
    His work has resulted in <strong>40 peer-reviewed publications</strong>, including <strong>24 first- or corresponding-author papers</strong>, and <strong>3 granted invention patents</strong>.
  </p>
</div>
